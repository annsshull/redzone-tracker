Scientific Methodology & Algorithmic Foundation

Formulated for SIH Evaluators, Disaster Managers & Academic Juries.

1 Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) Multi-Hazard Zonation

Multi-hazard zonation is determined via a multi-criteria pairwise comparison matrix following Saaty’s AHP framework. Normalized principal eigenvector weights:

Slope: 28%
Rainfall: 24%
Elevation: 20%
Seismic: 16%
Soil/LULC: 12%

Consistency Ratio (CR) = 0.042 < 0.10, verifying strict mathematical consistency.

2 Composite Carrying Capacity Index (CCI)

An area’s safe threshold is computed as a composite function of essential resources:

CCI = 0.25·Water + 0.20·Land + 0.20·Infrastructure + 0.15·EcoSensitivity + 0.20·PopPressure

Zones with CCI < 0.30 are designated as Critically Deficient, where current population exceeds environmental resilience.

3 MCDA Relocation Priority Engine

Priority Score = 0.30·Hazard + 0.25·CC_Overflow + 0.25·Vulnerability + 0.20·PopAtRisk_norm

Habitations scoring ≥ 0.75 are flagged for Immediate Tier 1 Relocation and matched to verified Safe Green Havens using Weighted Euclidean Spatial Distance minimizing transit hazard.

4 Government Policy Alignment

Fully integrated with PMAY-G (rural housing grants), NDRF/SDRF (state and national disaster relief norms), and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction (Target G: Multi-hazard early warning and risk assessment).
